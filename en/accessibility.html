Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 30, 2026

At Vision AI Studio we attach great importance to providing equal service to all visitors, and we work to make this website accessible and comfortable to use for people with disabilities as well.

Level of accessibility

The website has been made accessible in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and with the recommendations of Israeli Standard SI 5568, which is based on the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at Level AA. The website has been tested in common browsers, on desktop and on mobile.

Accessibility adjustments on the website

  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clear indication of the element in focus.
  • A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Adequate color contrast between text and background.
  • A hierarchical heading structure, labels for form fields and clear error messages.
  • Alternative text for images and descriptions for embedded content.
  • Language markup for text in another language, and a notice for links that open in a new window.
  • A button to pause the background video at the top of the page; video and animations stop automatically for anyone who has enabled "Reduce motion" on their device.
  • Full adaptation for viewing on mobile phones.

The accessibility menu

An accessibility button in the corner of the screen lets you: enlarge text, switch to high contrast, highlight links, switch to a readable font, and stop animations and video. Your settings are saved for your next visits to the website.

Accessibility limitations

  • The work videos on the website are visual films with no speech and no audio information, and therefore have no captions.
  • The websites section shows previews of external websites we built for clients. Those websites are not controlled by this website, and their level of accessibility is the responsibility of their owners.
  • Despite our efforts, some parts of the website may not yet be fully accessible. If you run into any difficulty, we would be glad to hear from you.

Accessibility inquiries

Have you run into a problem, or do you have a suggestion for improvement? We would be glad to hear from you and to take care of it as soon as possible.
